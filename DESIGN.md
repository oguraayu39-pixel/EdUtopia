# Visual direction — The Living Explorer’s Atlas

The references contribute four distinct ideas: the RPG screenshot suggests a persistent companion and dialogue, the forest scene suggests travel and discovery, the illustrated map suggests expressive cartography, and the planet suggests the scale of a larger world. The result uses original artwork, not copies of those images.

## Palette and material

- Deep teal `#174B46`: the ocean, navigation, and primary actions.
- Warm parchment `#F4E9CE`: dialogue, journal pages, and work surfaces.
- Antique gold `#DBA858`: destination markers, exploration cues, and welcome action.
- Forest green: systems, progress, and completed discoveries.
- Muted coral and rose: rooftops, character detail, and gentle feedback.

Libre Caslon Display lends large, literary headings. DM Sans keeps controls and learning text readable. Georgia and Arial remain available when web fonts do not load. Fine ink rules, subtle grain, small coordinates, a compass rose, and paper-colored overlays form the visual vocabulary.

## Screen composition

The opening is a full-bleed dark atlas with an illuminated globe and a warm invitation. The globe is intentionally an imaginative atlas rather than a precise physical model. The Addis Ababa chapter shifts into sunlight, gardens, market awnings, and urban rooftops. Location markers and a moving explorer are interactive overlays. Journal pages use calm space and short readable lessons. The owl companion Atlas carries a tiny open book, relating guidance to discovery.

Desktop layouts offer two columns and a persistent map journal. Narrow layouts stack content and move the map journal below the destinations. Motion is slow and atmospheric; reduced-motion preferences suppress animation.

## Original image generation

Built-in ImageGen was used. Final project assets: `dist/assets/atlas.webp` and `dist/assets/city.webp`. Both are derived from original generated PNGs, compressed to WebP for the app.

### Atlas prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: immersive full-bleed background for EDUTOPIA educational explorer web app. Create an original exquisitely detailed hand-painted antique fantasy world atlas seen from above, landscape 3:2. Deep desaturated petrol teal oceans with delicate ink wave etchings, warm cream parchment continents with olive forests, ochre mountains, fine engraved coastline, tiny whimsical towns, two small sailing ships, winding rivers, subtle longitude lines. Arrangement loosely recognizable Earth centered Africa and Europe with America on left and Asia on right, not precise scientific geography. Sophisticated vintage children's adventure book mixed with painterly game concept art; fine illustrative detail, paper texture, warm sunlight, atmospheric teal haze at outer edges. Lower left delicate antique compass rose. Leave center left ocean somewhat open for text overlay. No text, letters, labels, watermarks, UI, borders or interface. Flat map, no globe.

### City prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: game city map background. Create an original panoramic hand-painted illustrated fantasy cartography map of a fictionalized Addis Ababa neighborhood, Ethiopia. Beautiful warm ochre parchment terrain, rolling Ethiopian green highland hills on upper horizon, dense charming clusters of small colorful coral ochre teal-roofed urban buildings, jacaranda trees, eucalyptus trees, branching curving pale walking lanes. An open-air produce market on left middle with terracotta and striped ochre awnings and baskets, a friendly community waste sorting facility on right middle with green bins and roofed sorting shed, small town gardens, tiny pedestrians. Bird's eye 2.5D isometric view, richly detailed fine ink lines and watercolor/gouache, sophisticated vintage storybook atlas meets cozy adventure video game. Deep forest green foliage and warm earthy gold palette, soft afternoon sun, entirely illustrated, no photography. Wide landscape composition. No text or labels or UI. Leave lanes and some open park spaces for interactive markers.

## Middle school update
The supplied owl portrait appears in Atlas dialogues. Market objects use original illustrated stickers. The spinning globe projects the Earth texture from https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg onto a shaded sphere. The flight turns toward Addis Ababa before zooming into the illustrated city. Reduced motion uses a short static transition.
